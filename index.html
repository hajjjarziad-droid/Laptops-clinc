How the clinic works

Four steps, every laptop, no exceptions

The same intake-to-delivery pipeline runs whether a machine is fresh from the box or coming back to health.

01

Intake

Every unit is logged, brand-checked and matched against our clinic checklist.

02

Diagnostic

Battery, keyboard, screen, ports and thermals are stress-tested and scored.

03

Certify or Repair

New units are sealed as-is; used units are repaired and re-tested until they pass.

04

Deliver

Packed with a health report and the clinic's 90-day warranty card.
